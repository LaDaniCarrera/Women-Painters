## Problématique

Due to Mexico’s sociopolitical context during the nineteenth century, locating artworks or documentation related to individuals in general can be challenging. Research becomes considerably easier for the twentieth century, however both cases share the particularity of mostly containing men names; several of the women artists mentioned in this repository are not directly listed under the categories "Mexican artists” or "Mexican painters" in certain databases, or are overshadowed by the extensive documentation available on male painters.
It is therefore valuable to have a repository that brings together the names of women artists and explores the possible connections between them, just as professional relationships and collaborations among male painters of the same period have been consistently documented.

## Questions

- Did any of these artists studied or lived abroad?
- Were they affiliated to any political parties or groups?
- Did their work include similar themes between them?
- Where any of them maintained a teacher-student relationship?
- How many of them had a male teacher or were endorsed by another male artist?
- Were any of them from the same region?
- Did they practice any other techniques other than painting?
- What was their level of education? Were their families well put economically?
- Which were the most common themes approached by them through art?
- Were any manifestos or texts released by these artists?
