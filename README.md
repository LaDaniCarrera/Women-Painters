# Mexican female painters (XIX - XX century)

Women’s contributions and active work in art has a long history of dismissiveness. It was not too long ago that curators and historians started to question their actual importance and influence in different art movements, and it was not that different in the case of Mexico. This projects pretends to compile women who’s artistic practice takes place between the XIX and the XX century, their connections between them, what topics they would explore and their differences in  socio-economical grounds - which most likely played a part on their topics, and on the possibilities - or lack there of- to have their work being taken seriously.
